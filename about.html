About Phoenix Electronics Recycling

Our History

Phoenix Electronics Recycling has been committed to responsible e-waste recycling for more than 20 years, originally founded in Phoenix, Arizona, before expanding our services to Southern California. With decades of experience, we've built a reputation for secure, compliant, and environmentally responsible electronics recycling for homes, businesses, and large organizations.

Our Mission

At Phoenix Electronics Recycling, our mission is to make eco-friendly electronics recycling easy and accessible for everyone. We help individuals and businesses safely recycle e-waste—like computers, laptops, phones, TVs, and electronics accessories—so valuable materials can be reused and harmful waste stays out of landfills. By providing reliable e-waste pick-ups, drop-offs, and responsible recycling services, we're building a greener community and supporting a cleaner, sustainable future for our planet.

Make a Difference

Every device we recycle together helps protect the planet. By choosing Phoenix Electronics Recycling, you're keeping harmful e-waste out of landfills, saving reusable materials, and supporting a cleaner community. Small actions add up—your recycling makes a real difference.
