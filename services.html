Services

Data Destruction & Secure Recycling

At Phoenix Electronics Recycling, we provide secure data destruction services to permanently erase sensitive information from your devices. Our practices not only ensure your privacy and compliance but also promote environmental responsibility.

Hard drives, solid state drives, phones, memory cards and backup media are all handled the same way—your data never leaves with the device.

E-Waste Pickup

We come to you. Scheduled pickups for homes, offices and large organizations across the Inland Empire of California and Phoenix, Arizona.

Drop-Off Recycling

Bring your electronics to us at 1816 Duke St, Redlands during business hours. No appointment needed for household quantities.

Office & Business Decommission

Clearing an office, a server room or a full facility? We handle bulk removal of computers, monitors, servers, networking gear and office electronics.

Responsible Recycling

Recovered materials go back into use and hazardous components stay out of landfills.

  • Resource Conservation — metals and plastics recovered and reused
  • Landfill Reduction — toxins kept out of soil and water
  • Energy Savings — far less energy than mining new material
